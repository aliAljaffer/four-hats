Why identity needs storage.

  • Stable identity matters. Backup and discovery need a fixed name.
  • Claims survive Pod death. A new Pod reattaches the same storage.
  • Templates scale by copy. Each replica template mints its own claim.

06:00 · LimitsWatch out

Limits and tradeoffs

Learning disks. Claim lifetime.

  • Minikube storage is learning only. Deleting the cluster deletes the data.
  • Claims outlive the workload. Deleting the StatefulSet does not delete claims.
  • Production prefers managed options. Operators and backups sit outside this course.
  • PGDATA must be empty. The volume root holds lost plus found.
Tradeoff. StatefulSet dexterity trades against managed simplicity. Knowing both matters.
